Why Partnerships Matter

A Bridge Built on Shared Purpose.

GRACE was founded to connect Chinese American families with the broader Roslyn community — and no organization does that alone. Our partners include local businesses, civic groups, schools, and youth organizations.

Whether it's sponsoring an event, donating space or supplies, or co-hosting a cultural celebration, every partnership helps GRACE serve more families and build a more connected Roslyn.

Ways to Partner

Event Sponsorship
Support the Annual Gala, Lunar New Year, or Dragon Boat Festival with sponsorship that puts your name in front of the community.
In-Kind Support
Donate venue space, catering, printing, or supplies that help our events and programs run smoothly.

Volunteer Partnership
Mobilize employees, members, or students to volunteer alongside GRACE at events throughout the year.
